## Section I — From Zenana to Mardana: Satirical Inversion in Ladyland

> **[Author's Entry Section I — Student Essay Prompt (~200–250 words)]**
> Replace this block in `src/content/essays/04-speculative-wombs-utopia.md` with your analysis of Begum Rokeya Sakhawat Hossain's pioneering 1905 feminist utopia *Sultana's Dream*.
>
> - **Focus Point A:** Examine how Sister Sara dismantles the patriarchal rationale for *purdah* (seclusion in the *zenana*) by placing men inside the *mardana* while women govern public space.
> - **Focus Point B:** Highlight Hossain's vision of feminist technoscience—solar heat collectors, rain-storage balloons, electric agriculture, and a two-hour workday built on female university education.
